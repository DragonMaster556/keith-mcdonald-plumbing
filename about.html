About Us

Leaky pipes? Stopped up toilets? Full Septic Tanks?

If so, call Keith McDonald Plumbing, Sewer & Septic. We operate primarily in Washington, Baldwin, and Laurens Counties and surrounding Middle Georgia. Plumbing is our specialty and we maintain a dedicated staff of qualified technicians who quickly respond to your service calls.

We are equipped to take on tasks of any size. Our work history spans residential developments, commercial centers, medical complexes, and educational institutions.

At Keith McDonald Plumbing, we honor our promise of “Quality, Dependable Service.” We strive to maintain the high reputation we have built across our community so we are always the first name you trust for your plumbing solutions.
