Careers with Keith McDonald Plumbing

Looking for a rewarding, steady career with competitive pay and comprehensive hands-on training? Keith McDonald Plumbing is continually seeking dedicated trade professionals.

Licensed Service Plumbers

Manage residential and commercial service calls with company-supplied vehicles, professional tools, and dispatched routes.

Apprentice Technicians

Learn alongside Master Plumbers with full hands-on mentoring, trade licensing assistance, and growth milestones.

Equipment Operators

Operate backhoes, trenchers, and excavators for septic system installations and main sewer line replacements.
